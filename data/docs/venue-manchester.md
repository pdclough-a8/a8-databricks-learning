# Manchester venue: Mill Yard Studios

## About the room
Mill Yard Studios is our Manchester venue. The main studio seats 120 people, with a raised stage, a projector, a
hearing loop and a separate breakout room for workshop sessions.

## Getting there
The venue is a fifteen-minute walk from the main railway station, or a short tram ride to the stop nearest the
canal. The entrance is through the courtyard gate, which is signposted from the canal path. The courtyard is lit and
the studio door is on the left.

## Arriving
Registration opens 45 minutes before the start in the courtyard foyer. Cycle racks are inside the courtyard. There is
no event parking, but a public car park is a five-minute walk away.
