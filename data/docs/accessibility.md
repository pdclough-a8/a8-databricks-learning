# Accessibility

## Step-free access
All four in-person venues have step-free access from the street to the main room, and accessible toilets on the same
floor. Tell us before the event if you use a wheelchair or mobility aid, so we can reserve space and plan seating.

## Hearing and sight
Rooms have a hearing loop at the front. Slides are available to download before every event, in large print on
request. Online events have live captions on the stream.

## Quiet space and breaks
A quiet room is available at in-person events for anyone who needs a break. Sessions include a break at least every
90 minutes, and you can leave and return to any session.

## Assistance and carers
A carer or support worker attending with you does not need a ticket. Tell the events team their name before the event
so they can be welcomed at registration. Assistance animals are welcome at every venue.
