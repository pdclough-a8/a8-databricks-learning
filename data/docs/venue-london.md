# London venue: Riverside Hall

## About the room
Riverside Hall is our London venue and our largest in-person room. The hall seats 150 people theatre style, with a
projector, a hearing loop and a stage that is level with the floor for wheelchair users.

## Getting there
The hall is a six-minute walk from the nearest Underground station, along the river path. The entrance is on the
river side of the building, under a glass canopy, and is the only entrance used for events. Staff in A8 shirts meet
guests from 45 minutes before the start.

## Arriving
Registration is in the entrance hall. Security may ask to see your confirmation email, so keep it open on your phone.
There is no event parking, and the area has limited street parking, so we recommend public transport.
