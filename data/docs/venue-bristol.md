# Bristol venue: Harbourside Rooms

## About the room
Harbourside Rooms is our Bristol venue and our smallest in-person room. The main room seats 60 people around tables,
with a projector and a hearing loop, which makes it well suited to workshops and hands-on sessions.

## Getting there
The venue is a twelve-minute walk from the main railway station, following the harbour. The entrance is at the end
of the quay, next to the ferry landing, up a short ramp. There is a lift to the first-floor room.

## Arriving
Registration opens 45 minutes before the start at the foot of the ramp. There is no event parking, and the nearest
public car park is a ten-minute walk, so please allow extra time.
