# Online events: A8 Live

## How online events work
Online events run on A8 Live, our streaming platform, and can take up to 300 people. Your joining link is in your
confirmation email and opens 15 minutes before the start. You can watch in a web browser and no software is needed.

## If the video buffers or freezes
First check your connection, then close other programs and browser tabs that use the internet. Lower the stream
quality using the settings icon, which uses less bandwidth. If the picture still freezes, refresh the page; you will
rejoin at the live point. Using a cable rather than wireless often helps.

## If the audio drops out
Check that your device volume is on and that the right output device is selected in your browser. Refresh the page if
the sound stops. If the audio fails for more than two minutes, use the chat to tell the moderator, who can check the
stream from our side.

## Chat, questions and recordings
The chat is moderated, and questions put in the chat are read out in the question session. A recording is sent to
everyone who booked, within three working days, whether or not they joined live.
