# Cancellations and refunds

## When you cancel a booking
You can cancel a booking from your confirmation email. A cancelled booking releases its seats straight away, so
they can be offered to people on the waiting list. Cancelling one ticket in a booking of several keeps the rest.

## How much you are refunded
If you cancel 14 or more days before the event starts, you receive a full refund. If you cancel 7 to 13 days before
the event, you receive a 50% refund. If you cancel less than 7 days before the event, there is no refund, but you can
transfer the ticket to a colleague instead. Complimentary and free tickets have no refund because nothing was paid.

## When A8 cancels or changes an event
If A8 cancels an event, every ticket holder receives a full refund or can move to the next suitable date. If we move
an event to a different date or venue, you can keep your booking or cancel for a full refund, whatever the usual
refund period. We tell ticket holders by email as soon as a decision is made.

## How long refunds take
Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 10 working days of the cancellation.
