# Leeds venue: The Engine House

## About the room
The Engine House is our Leeds venue. The main room seats 80 people in rows or around tables, and has a projector,
a hearing loop and wireless microphones.

## Getting there
The venue is a ten-minute walk from Leeds railway station. Leave by the main concourse exit and follow the signs for
the river. The entrance is on the quieter side street, not on the main road, behind a green door beside the old
loading bay. Look for the A8 banner at the door.

## Arriving
Registration opens 45 minutes before the start. Cloakroom space is limited, so please bring only what you need.
There is no event parking, but there are public car parks within five minutes' walk.
