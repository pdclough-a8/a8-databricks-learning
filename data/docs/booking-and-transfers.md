# Booking, confirmation and ticket transfers

## Making a booking
You can book up to 10 tickets in one booking. Each ticket is one seat. Tickets are held for 30 minutes while you
complete the booking form, and the booking is confirmed when payment or a complimentary code has been accepted.
Student, early bird and complimentary tickets are shown on the booking form when they apply to the event.

## Your confirmation email
A confirmation email is sent within one hour of a confirmed booking. It contains your booking reference, the event
date and the joining instructions. If you have not received it after two hours, check your spam folder, then contact
the events team with your booking reference and we will resend it.

## Transferring a ticket to a colleague
You can transfer any ticket to a colleague up to 48 hours before the event starts. Reply to your confirmation email
with the colleague's name and email address and the events team will update the booking. A transfer is free and does
not change the ticket type, so a student or complimentary ticket can only be transferred to someone who qualifies for
the same ticket type. Name changes within 48 hours of the start must be made at the registration desk.

## Joining the waiting list
If an event is full you can join the waiting list from its booking page. Places are offered in the order people
joined. When a place is offered you have 24 hours to accept it before it is offered to the next person.
