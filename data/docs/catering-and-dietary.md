# Catering and dietary needs

## What is provided
In-person events include arrival coffee, a lunch and afternoon refreshments. Online events do not include catering.
Lunch is a buffet with at least one vegetarian main course. Drinks include tea, coffee, water and a choice of soft
drinks.

## Dietary requirements
Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free and nut-free meals are available. Tell us your requirement at least
7 days before the event by replying to your confirmation email. Requirements made at the event cannot always be met,
because meals are ordered from the confirmed requirements.

## How many meals we order
We order meals from the number of confirmed bookings with a small allowance for people who do not attend. If you
cancel, your meal is removed from the order. If lunch runs short, tell a member of the events team and we will bring
extra food as soon as we can.

## Allergies
We list the main allergens for every dish on the lunch table. We cannot guarantee that food is free of traces of
allergens. If you have a severe allergy, speak to the events team before the event.
